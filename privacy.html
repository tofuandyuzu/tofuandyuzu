Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Tofu & Yuzu ("the app") is a personal publishing tool. It is used only by its owner to upload Tofu & Yuzu videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What the app accesses

The app signs in to the owner's accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok with permission to upload videos and their captions. It does not read, collect or store data about any other person.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens the platforms issue are kept privately on the owner's own systems. They are not shared with or sold to anyone, and they are used only to publish the owner's videos.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings. Once access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted.
